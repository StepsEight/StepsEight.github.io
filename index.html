About me

I study distributed fiber-optic sensing for traffic and ocean monitoring at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My earlier work focused on perimeter security and intrusion identification.

My research interests include distributed acoustic sensing, traffic monitoring, and ocean monitoring. I am interested in using optical fiber infrastructure to observe the physical environment.

Before my Ph.D., I studied at Chongqing University (M.S. in Communication and Information Engineering; 2021.09–2024.07) and Shantou University (B.S. in Communication Engineering; 2017.09–2021.07). More about my background.
